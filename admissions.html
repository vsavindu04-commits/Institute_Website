Admissions

Join Horizon Institute and take the next step in your career.

Admission Requirements

  • Students from the NVQ system, as well as those from the G.C.E. A/L (Advanced Level) or G.C.E. O/L (Ordinary Level) streams, will be eligible for admission to these courses.
  • Basic English Knowledge
  • Required Documents
    • Copy of Birth Certificate
    • Copies of Educational Certificates (G.C.E. O/L and A/L Results)
    • Other Relevant Educational Documents
    • Copy of National Identity Card (NIC) or Passport

Application Process

  1. Check Eligibility Verify that you meet the admission requirements before applying.
  2. Online Application Submit your application through the official website.
  3. Interview Phase Selected candidates may be invited for a formal interview.
  4. Final Registration Selected candidates must complete document verification and fee payment on the specified date to formally enroll.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the available intakes?
We have three intakes per year: January, May, and September.
Is financial aid available?
Yes, we offer scholarships and flexible payment plans based on merit and need.
Can I transfer credits from another institution?
Yes, credits are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Please contact admissions for details.
